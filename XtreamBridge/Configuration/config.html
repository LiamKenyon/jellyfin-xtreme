Xtream Bridge

Connect your panel, choose categories and watch through Jellyfin.

Category changes take effect after saving. Run “Refresh Guide” and “Refresh Internet Channels” in Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks afterwards. The guide also refreshes on Jellyfin’s schedule.

Live TV categories

VOD categories

New categories are enabled by default. Turn off any categories you do not want available to your household. These switches are server-wide, not per-user parental controls.

Credentials are stored in Jellyfin’s plugin configuration. Protect your server configuration and backups. VOD appears as “Xtream Movies”; series, catch-up and provider recording are not included in this release.
