Collections appear in Jellyfin’s Collections view. They do not add category tabs to every client’s Live TV screen. This plugin only removes collection members it previously added.
Programme guide
Choose the provider or alternative source per channel in the channel editor. Source priority is explicit, avoiding duplicated listings.
Playback & DVR
Changing DVR mode reimports channels under a different backend. Existing favourites or recording schedules may need remapping. The plugin preserves unrelated tuners. Set the recording directory, permissions and retention in Jellyfin’s Live TV settings. Native tuner connection limits and stream sharing apply in DVR mode. DVR channels receive simple sequential numbers unless you specify custom numbers.
Catch-up requires provider archive support and retained guide listings; it is not local recording. Pause/rewind behaviour depends on the client and Jellyfin buffering. No guaranteed long-term timeshift buffer is supplied.
Sync & diagnostics
